# Leonardo Freitas

I build AI automations that don't break: quote desks, lead intake, CRM workflows and n8n, self-hosted or cloud. Based in Brazil, working remotely (UTC-3).

## What I build

- **Quote desks**: request emails come in, a priced draft comes out, and a person approves it before anything is sent.
- **Lead intake**: forms, email and chat go into your CRM, deduplicated, with the follow-up started.
- **n8n workflows**: self-hosted or cloud, with retries, an error queue and alerts.
- **Automation rescue**: fixing workflows that fail silently, then monitoring them.
- **Systems and internal tools**: small apps and dashboards built around how your team already works.

## How I work

- Numbers and business rules live in tested code, not in the AI.
- A person approves anything that reaches a customer.
- Every error has an exit: retries, an error queue and an alert.
- I build with AI agents (Claude Code) under my direction: I set the rules, check the results and decide what ships.
